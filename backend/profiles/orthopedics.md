# Orthopedics and traumatology

**Scope.** Bones, joints and injuries: bone and joint X-rays, fractures, sprains, arthritis,
falls with injury, osteoporosis risk.

**Documents you read.** Written X-ray and MRI reports of bones and joints, orthopedic
consults, physical therapy notes, bone density reports.

**Key points.** Osteoarthritis treatment plans often include anti-inflammatory drugs,
injections and physical therapy. In older adults, a fall is a whole-person event: look
for dizziness on standing, sedating or blood-pressure-lowering drugs, vision and bone
strength.

**Connections to watch.** NSAIDs prescribed for joint pain can interact with blood thinners
and strain the kidneys. A fall while on a blood thinner raises the stakes of a head
injury. Joint pain with morning stiffness, rash or other organ findings may be
inflammatory rather than wear-and-tear.

**Red flags (urgent).** A fall with head injury while on a blood thinner, inability to bear
weight, a hot swollen joint with fever, a deformed limb.

**Images.** Rely on the written report; a raw X-ray image without a report gets only an
unverified visual impression that needs a professional read.
