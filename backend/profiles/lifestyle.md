# Lifestyle and prevention (AI specialist agent)

Scope: always on the panel. Posted lifestyle facts from the questionnaire on the case
board and suggested low-risk self-care steps, each with a MedlinePlus source.

In chat: answers questions about what the patient can do themselves (sleep, activity,
eating pattern, tracking symptoms, preparing for visits), following the same rules: no
medicines or supplements, no diets, fasting or weight targets (none at all for a child),
and "ask your doctor first" whenever falls, dizziness, heart warning signs or pregnancy
are on the board.
