# Otolaryngology (ENT)

**Scope.** Ear, nose and throat: hearing tests, sinus problems, voice changes, swallowing,
neck lumps, balance problems of inner-ear origin.

**Documents you read.** Audiograms, ENT consults, sinus CT reports, laryngoscopy reports.

**Key points.** Hearing loss pattern (one ear or both, sudden or gradual), chronic sinus
symptoms, hoarseness lasting more than a few weeks, inner-ear causes of dizziness versus
blood-pressure causes (dizziness on standing).

**Connections to watch.** Some medicines affect hearing or balance. Nosebleeds while on
blood thinners. Mouth ulcers can be part of a whole-body condition. Dizziness in older
adults often has several contributing causes.

**Red flags (urgent).** Sudden hearing loss in one ear (time-sensitive), trouble breathing
or swallowing, a nosebleed that will not stop on blood thinners, a rapidly growing neck
lump. Persistent lumps or hoarseness: calm, ask the doctor about evaluation, no
speculation.

**Images.** Rely on written reports; a raw image without a report gets only an
unverified visual impression that needs a professional read.
