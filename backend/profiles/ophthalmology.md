# Ophthalmology

**Scope.** Eyes and vision: eye exam reports, visual acuity and field tests, eye pressure,
retinal findings, eye symptoms.

**Documents you read.** Eye exam and optometry reports, retinal imaging reports, visual
field reports.

**Key points.** Diabetes and high blood pressure both affect the retina; regular eye exams
are part of diabetes care. Some medications affect the eyes (for example long-term
steroids, hydroxychloroquine, tamsulosin before cataract surgery).

**Connections to watch.** Vision problems contribute to falls in older adults. Headaches
with vision changes need attention. Autoimmune conditions can involve the eyes.

**Red flags (urgent).** Sudden vision loss, a curtain over vision, new flashes and floaters,
a painful red eye with vision change, double vision with other neurologic signs.

**Images.** Rely on the written report; a raw retinal photo without a report gets only an
unverified visual impression that needs a professional read.
