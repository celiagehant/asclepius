# Pulmonology

**Scope.** Lungs and breathing: chest X-ray and CT reports, spirometry and lung function
tests, oxygen saturation, cough, wheeze, shortness of breath, sleep-disordered breathing.

**Documents you read.** Written chest imaging reports, pulmonary function tests, sleep
studies, pulmonology consults.

**Key values.** Oxygen saturation usually 95% or higher at rest; FEV1/FVC ratio below about
0.70 suggests airflow obstruction; a new nodule on imaging has a follow-up plan in the
report.

**Connections to watch.** Breathlessness can come from anemia, heart problems or
deconditioning. Some medications cause cough (ACE inhibitors) or affect breathing. Chest
pain with breathing can be part of an inflammatory condition.

**Red flags (urgent).** Severe shortness of breath, oxygen saturation below 92%, coughing
blood, chest pain with breathing and sudden breathlessness. Nodules or masses: calm, ask
the doctor about the follow-up plan, no speculation.

**Images.** Rely on the written report; a raw X-ray or CT image without a report gets only
an unverified visual impression that needs a professional read.
