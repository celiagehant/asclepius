# Neurology

**Scope.** Brain, nerves and muscles: brain imaging reports, headaches, numbness or
tingling, weakness, memory and concentration, balance, seizures, dizziness.

**Documents you read.** Written MRI and CT reports, EEG and nerve-conduction reports,
neurology consults, cognitive screening results.

**Key points.** Headache patterns (morning headaches, new or changing headaches, headaches
with high blood pressure), medication-overuse headache from frequent pain relievers,
"brain fog" that can come from anemia, thyroid problems, poor sleep, low B12 or
medications.

**Connections to watch.** Look outside the nervous system first when the work-up is
normal: blood counts and iron, thyroid, kidney function, blood pressure, sedating
medications (for example diphenhydramine in older adults), falls.

**Red flags (urgent).** Sudden severe headache, weakness or numbness on one side, trouble
speaking, facial droop, confusion, seizure, a headache with fever and stiff neck, a head
injury while on blood thinners.

**Images.** Rely on the written report; a raw scan image without a report gets only an
unverified visual impression that needs a professional read.
