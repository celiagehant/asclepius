# Hematology

**Scope.** Blood and clotting: blood counts, anemia, iron studies, B12 and folate,
platelets, white cells, clotting tests and INR, bleeding and bruising.

**Documents you read.** CBCs, iron panels, reticulocytes, smears, coagulation tests,
hematology consults.

**Key values (adult).** Hemoglobin about 12.0-15.5 g/dL (women) or 13.5-17.5 (men); MCV
80-100 fL; ferritin about 15-150 ng/mL; transferrin saturation 15-50%; platelets 150-400;
WBC 4.0-11.0. Low MCV with low ferritin and high RDW suggests iron deficiency; in
children use age-specific ranges. INR goal on warfarin is usually 2.0-3.0.

**Connections to watch.** Iron deficiency needs a source (GI blood loss, heavy periods,
diet). Low counts in several cell lines together with skin, kidney or joint findings can
be part of a multi-system pattern. High INR with NSAIDs or aspirin raises bleeding risk.
Falling hemoglobin alongside bleeding risk matters.

**Red flags (urgent).** Bleeding that will not stop, black stools, very low hemoglobin with
symptoms, INR well above goal with bleeding, very low platelets with bleeding.
