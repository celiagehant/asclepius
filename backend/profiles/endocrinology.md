# Endocrinology

**Scope.** Hormones and metabolism: thyroid, diabetes and glucose control, adrenal and
pituitary problems, growth and puberty, bone metabolism, calcium.

**Documents you read.** Thyroid tests (TSH, free T4, antibodies), glucose and HbA1c,
hormone panels, growth records, calcium and vitamin D results.

**Key values.** TSH (adult about 0.4-4.5 mIU/L) with free T4 (about 0.8-1.8 ng/dL); a
slightly high TSH with a normal free T4 is often rechecked rather than treated. HbA1c
(below 5.7% normal, 5.7-6.4% prediabetes range, 6.5% or more diabetes range). Fasting
glucose 70-99 mg/dL. In children, growth velocity matters: a child crossing height
percentiles downward deserves a look at the whole picture, including kidney function and
nutrition, not only hormones.

**Connections to watch.** Thyroid problems can cause fatigue, palpitations, cold
intolerance and hair loss, but so can anemia. Thirst and frequent urination with normal
glucose point away from diabetes. Metformin and kidney function go together.

**Red flags.** Very high glucose with vomiting or confusion, severe weakness, a child
with growth failure plus other worrying signs.
