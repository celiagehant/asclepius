ΛSCLEPIUS · design proposal

A mockup to review before anything changes in the app. Greek, medical and hospital style: marble white, deep navy, a touch of gold, classical serif headings, a Greek key (meander) border, and a new emblem with a many-headed serpent.

1 · The new emblem: one serpent, many heads

The serpent is the classical symbol of medicine and pharmacy. Ours has three heads on one body: many specialties, one patient, a holistic view of the whole person. The home page would explain it in one sentence. Pick one (or ask for changes).

Recommended

A · The medallion

A Greek-coin medallion: navy with a gold rim, a staff, and one serpent rising into three heads (gold). Reads well even tiny, as the browser icon.

Pharmacy

B · The bowl of Hygieia

The pharmacy symbol (Hygieia, Asclepius' daughter, goddess of health): a bowl with a serpent whose three heads lean over it. Strong link to pharmacy and prevention.

C · Rod and laurel

A clean line drawing: the rod with a three-headed serpent in blue and a gold laurel at the base. Lightest and most modern.
